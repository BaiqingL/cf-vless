# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Disagreement is welcome; harassment,
threats, discrimination, doxxing, and disclosure of another person's credentials
or private infrastructure are not.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- explaining technical tradeoffs with evidence;
- assuming good faith while asking for clarification;
- respecting different levels of experience; and
- helping keep issues focused and reproducible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate
this standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns through the repository's private security-advisory
channel, which also serves as the temporary private maintainer channel. Do not
include credentials, private hostnames, client configurations, account
identifiers, traffic captures, or personal information in a public issue.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith, limit disclosure to people needed
to resolve the concern, and avoid conflicts of interest where practical.
